    Roadmap to the platinum

    The order to earn them in, built from the official descriptions: play the story first, then the strangers and friends, then activities and challenges, then collectibles, then money — and clean up 100% last. The platinum unlocks when the other 54 are done.

    Missable trophies — do these in time

    Only a few trophies can be missed, and they are flagged here from the official descriptions. Everything else can be earned any time after its content unlocks. The exact missions are not yet verified in the game — treat the flags as a warning, not a promise.

    Easy to miss, not missable: “The Truth Is Out There” (a strange hidden moment — keep an eye out while exploring) and “Bad Luck, I Guess” (a second attempt pays off). Neither is tied to a one-time story beat in its description, so both can be chased after the story.
